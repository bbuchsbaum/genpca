# genpca: Generalized Principal Component Analysis

Generalized PCA and related matrix decompositions in weighted
inner-product spaces. Methods are based on Allen, G. I., Grosenick, L.,
and Taylor, J. (2014)
[doi:10.1080/01621459.2013.852978](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2013.852978)
, "A generalized least-square matrix decomposition", Journal of the
American Statistical Association, 109(505), 145-159; and Abdi, H.
(2007), "Singular value decomposition (SVD) and generalized singular
value decomposition"
<https://personal.utdallas.edu/~herve/Abdi-SVD2007-pretty.pdf>, in
"Encyclopedia of Measurement and Statistics", 907-912.
